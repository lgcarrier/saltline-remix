# Dust assets and provenance

The user-supplied `reference/de_dust_cs16.zip` contains the GoldSrc BSP, OBJ/MTL, source texture images and overview. Its map title credits Dave Johnston. The original geometry is transformed into `src/dust-map.json`; the archive remains unmodified. Attached archive prose is treated as source/reference data, not task instructions.

The original Saltline engine, weapon, robot enemies, procedural prop/material constructors, animation, effects and synthesized audio remain in the standalone bundle. Shared Assault physics, material support and GPU resource warmup are reused. The original and Assault maps remain separately available.

Nine deterministic desert albedo/normal/ORM sets are authored in `src/dust-materials.js`. RGB albedo is sRGB; normal and ORM maps are linear. ORM packs occlusion, roughness and metalness into RGB. Tiling scale is in metres. Native source UVs are retained for door and shutter atlases. Architectural finish follows the actual source face charts and cover bounds.

`src/dust-dressing.js` adds source-aligned shutter frames, case hardware, segmented limestone coping and restrained plaster-loss details. Source floor checks support the low fallen fragments; decorative additions do not alter collision or portal widths. `src/dust-environment.js` supplies the clear sky, dry lighting, procedural distant settlement and eroded limestone masses. All backdrop vertices are checked against the source bounds plus an 8 m margin. These are authored procedural approximations, not scanned buildings or terrain.

`assets/dust/lime-plaster-albedo.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project and embedded into the Dust HTML. It is an authored synthetic texture, not a captured photogrammetry scan. Its returned size is 1254×1254. The exact generation prompt was:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: square seamless PBR base-color texture for a photorealistic desert first-person game, a 3 metre by 3 metre swatch of old pale warm ochre lime plaster on a North African sandstone building. Orthographic straight-on macro material scan, no perspective, no objects, no scene, no borders, no text, no watermark. Flat diffuse neutral light with zero directional shadows or baked highlights. The entire image is plaster: sun-bleached warm limestone ivory and sandy beige with naturally varied very fine mineral aggregate, restrained hand-trowelled texture, tiny pits, a few small areas of worn thin limewash revealing tan sandy substrate, extremely subtle age discoloration. Avoid bricks, courses, masonry joints, large cracks, stains with recognizable shapes, speckles covering the surface, over-contrast, blue tones. Quiet photogrammetry quality detail across macro, meso, micro scales; natural and believable with subtle tonal range. Must tile seamlessly horizontally and vertically. High resolution 2048 square.

`assets/dust/desert-road-albedo.png` was also created with the built-in imagegen tool, inspired by the supplied road's worn paving and sand. Its generation prompt was:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square game PBR base-color road texture, 3.2 metres square, high-resolution material scan. Primary request: recreate the worn desert road character of old Counter-Strike Dust as a natural photorealistic material: uneven small roughly squared warm limestone cobbles, 12 to 24 cm across, in irregular running courses, mostly buried under thin dry dusty sand. Some stones remain exposed in the centre; others fade softly beneath sand at the sides, but avoid any clear border or straight stripe. Colours are restrained pale ochre, buff, warm grey limestone, sandy beige; dust settles naturally in eroded joins. All-over detailed but quiet and low contrast, plausible rounded chipped stone edges, tiny aggregate, very shallow irregular seams, no large rocks or potholes. Orthographic straight down, flat neutral diffuse illumination, no sun, no shadows, no highlight, no perspective, no surroundings, no painted lines, no objects, no text. Entire image fills with this ground material. Seamless edges in both directions, no obvious repeating motif, no big cloud-shaped stains. Physically plausible photogrammetry appearance, highly detailed natural surface for real-time first-person graphics. Square 2048.
