## Scope and verdict

Image-only independent review of `v3-ct_approach.png`, `v3-market.png`, `v3-apartments.png`, `v3-house_exterior.png`, and `v3-house_interior.png` in `output/playwright/italy/`. Capture settings are 1600 × 900, DPR 1, high/native resolution, FOV 74, exposure 1. The capture owner reports floor, headroom and navigation checks in `v3-captures.json`; this review does not independently validate movement, collision, gameplay or performance. No code or browser was inspected. Pending changes after these captures are outside this verdict.

**The modern AAA photorealistic target remains unmet.** Surface clarity and consistency are visibly improved: the stone wall treatment, calmer plaster, clearer crates, crisp floral wallpaper and visible produce stalls make this a more coherent remaster. The supplied views still read as simple legacy architecture with detailed surface images, rather than convincing photographed construction and light.

## Per-frame assessment

- **CT approach:** strongest overall street composition. The fork, steps, central wedge façade, shutters and roofline retain a recognizable old-village identity. Improved stone helps, but flat construction detail and repeated paving remain conspicuous.
- **Market:** much stronger evidence than the first capture. The market stalls and produce now read clearly. Canopy material/form and produce shading are the leading local realism gaps.
- **Apartments:** the mission prop has more readable shape and material variety than the enclosure. The stone room lacks the material separation and lighting depth expected of a photorealistic interior. This view alone cannot certify the full apartment route.
- **House exterior:** coherent, unobstructed playable-view composition with recognizable crates, arched opening and emblem band. Construction detail, contact depth, façade weathering and arch smoothness remain below target. No detached-roof or ground-wedge claim is made for this capture.
- **House interior:** clear improvement in wallpaper and carpet texture readability. It still reads as a simply constructed legacy corridor, with limited secondary detail and localized lighting.
