**The photorealistic AAA target is still unmet.** V7 makes a useful improvement in exterior lighting depth and the CT windows. The remaining gap is visible construction and material continuity across large surfaces; a stronger sky and darker shadows alone do not resolve it.

## Bounded corrective priorities

1. **Keep v7's exterior directional contrast, but recover the shaded market materials.** Test a restrained warm bounce or localized ambient contribution under the canopy. Compare the left fruit bin and front wood rail at the same camera. Preserve the deep passage shadows; lifting the whole scene would erase the useful improvement. Acceptance: individual fruit, rail grain and bin joints remain readable in shade without making the canopy underside as bright as the sunlit pavement.
2. **Finish one complete piece of architecture at close gameplay scale.** Prioritize the house front and adjacent passage: believable wall-cap and corner profiles, dimensional arch stones following the existing aperture, distinct motif framing, and restrained repair/runoff variation tied to actual edges. Preserve the native silhouette, openings and movement clearances. Acceptance: the large house wall and arch stop reading as flat textured blocks in the existing exterior camera; small ornaments alone do not satisfy this.
3. **Resolve material-to-geometry contacts in a limited representative set.** Inspect the crate bases, stall legs, interior skirting and stone corners. Add or correct local joint depth/contact shading where it is visible, then compare at the original camera. Avoid broad dark outlines and blanket dirt. Acceptance: objects meet their supports and wall materials turn corners convincingly while retaining clear material detail.

The five frames show a coherent Italy remaster, with v7's CT approach its clearest improvement. They do not yet show consistent photographic construction, lighting and material depth across exterior and interior play spaces.

## V8 visual addendum — 2026-11-12

Reviewed all five matching v8 images against the v7 views above. This is a separate image assessment from the geometry audit.

- [CT approach](../output/playwright/italy/v8-ct_approach.png): the conspicuous rectangular plaster mismatch around the nearest window is largely resolved, and more distant windows read as inset objects. The foreground building still has an abrupt horizontal change in plaster texture/colour above the lower trim; it reads as an atlas boundary rather than a convincing repair or construction joint.
- [Market](../output/playwright/italy/v8-market.png): shaded fruit and the front wood rail recover some readable detail while the stronger sun/shade division survives. This partially resolves the v7 regression. The smooth canopy band and sparse surrounding wall remain visually simple.
- [House exterior](../output/playwright/italy/v8-house_exterior.png): separate coping stones, framed motifs and the returned corner give the building a more assembled appearance. The passage's stone arch now explains its angular inner opening more convincingly. These are useful improvements, although the expansive plaster field, repeated motifs and paving still dominate the impression of a remastered older environment.
- [Apartments](../output/playwright/italy/v8-apartments.png) and [house interior](../output/playwright/italy/v8-house_interior.png): no substantial improvement or new severe regression is visible. Flat stone corners, sparse room construction and broad repeated wallpaper/carpet remain the weakest parts of the five-view set.

**The photorealistic AAA gate remains open.** V8 improves construction and material continuity, but the principal remaining limit is inconsistent physical depth across whole surfaces and rooms. The next useful art correction is the visible CT plaster boundary, followed by representative interior wall/corner/skirting construction; another global contrast or sky adjustment would not address those deficits. No visual blocker newly introduced by v8 was identified in these five views.

## Final v10 image assessment — 2026-11-12

Viewed all five v10 frames. The [CT approach](../output/playwright/italy/v10-ct_approach.png) now resolves the earlier broad horizontal plaster mismatch. The triangular lighting patches visible in the intermediate v9 CT image are also gone. Window depth and the strong exterior sun/shade separation remain intact.

The [house interior](../output/playwright/italy/v10-house_interior.png) gains readable profiles at the wall/ceiling and wall/carpet junctions. This improves how the room is assembled, though repeated wallpaper, carpet and ceiling patterns still dominate. The [apartments view](../output/playwright/italy/v10-apartments.png) remains materially similar, with flat stone corners and weak equipment contact among its visible limitations.

The [market](../output/playwright/italy/v10-market.png) retains the recovered fruit and rail detail in shade; its large background wall is now more consistently shaded but still sparse. The [house exterior](../output/playwright/italy/v10-house_exterior.png) retains the improved coping, framed motifs and passage arch. Large uninterrupted surfaces and repeated paving remain obvious across these exterior views.

**No new severe rendering defect was identified in this final five-view set, but the requested photorealistic AAA finish is not established.** The concrete material seams and intermediate lighting regression have been addressed. The remaining gap concerns broader environment construction, surface variety and interior detail, rather than another isolated seam or shader correction. This visual verdict remains separate from the passing geometry and gameplay evidence.
