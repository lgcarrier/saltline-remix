**Modern AAA photorealism is not achieved in these images.** V4 improves several conspicuous v3 details, but broad featureless concrete areas now weaken the result. ## Visible improvements from v3

- **House exterior:** the arched opening has a smoother outline, façade trim reads somewhat more distinctly, and the bright speckled patch below the sill is no longer visible in this capture. This is a statement about the static frame, not temporal stability.
- **Apartments:** warmer illumination makes the rear wall, window and metal tower more readable and gives the enclosure better separation from the prop. The floor now has a distinct material appearance from the walls.
- **Market:** the foreground canopy no longer has the coarse, stone-like blue relief. Its hanging edge is less rigid, and the fruit has clearer, smoother highlights. These changes improve material identity.
- **CT approach:** directional shadows across the central façade and route are clear. The fork, steps, central wedge façade, shutters and stone base retain the strongest recognizable street composition.
- **House interior:** the sharp floral wallpaper and carpet treatment remains intact. No large new improvement is apparent from this particular frame.
