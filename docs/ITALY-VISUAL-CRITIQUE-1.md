# Italy visual critique — candidate 1

## Scope and verdict

Independent image-only review of five candidate captures at 1600 × 900, DPR 1, high/native resolution, FOV 74, exposure 1. No code, gameplay, collision, or performance inspection was performed. Capture positions have not been verified as reachable playable-floor positions; defects are observations of these supplied views, not claims about their cause or visibility on a normal player route.

**The modern AAA photorealistic target is not met.** These images read as a legacy map with enhanced surface detail and some newer props. Obvious detached geometry, flat architectural details, blurry close surfaces, repetition, and weak light/material separation remain visible. The CT approach is the strongest composition; the house exterior contains the most urgent defects.

## Capture issues and source fidelity limits

- `candidate-market.png`: the large red foreground panel blocks much of the frame and hides the market's defining arrangement. Add an unobstructed market overview and a produce/stall detail view. This image cannot establish market fidelity or overall dressing quality.
- `candidate-apartments.png`: the camera is pressed close to an exterior wall; the interior apartment route is not visible. Add views of the apartment entrance, interior passage and exit. This is an evidence gap, not proof that those spaces are absent.
- `candidate-ct_approach.png`: the narrow cobbled fork, stepped route, central wedge-shaped façade, shutters and plaster buildings provide the clearest old-village spatial identity. Preserve those masses, route widths and recognizable landmarks while improving their construction and materials.
- The provided top-down source overview helps establish the intended interconnected footprint, but five perspective views cannot prove complete topology, scale, room continuity or sightline fidelity. Matched source/candidate camera pairs and a corresponding candidate overhead are needed for that claim.
- The supplied Tsarvar page returned an error and the Znews image could not be fetched during this review; neither was used as visual evidence. The inspected source reference was `/tmp/cs_italy-reference.png`.
